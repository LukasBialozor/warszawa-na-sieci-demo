## Data

  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The derived database `public/data/city.json` is made available under
  the same license.
- `public/data/lod2.json`, `public/data/lod2.bin`, `data/landmarks/*_lod2.json` and the LOD2-derived parts of
  `public/models/pkin.glb` / `pkin.json` (the wings of the Palace of Culture and Science) are derived from the
  GUGiK "Budynki 3D LOD2" dataset (Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii, www.geoportal.gov.pl), shared free of
  charge under Polish geodetic law; source: GUGiK.
- The screenshots in `docs/` show the demo, i.e. a rendering of the data above.
